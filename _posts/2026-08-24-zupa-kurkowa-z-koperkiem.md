## Ingredients (2 people)

- 300 g chanterelles (cantharellen)
- 300 g potatoes
- 1 small carrot
- a small bunch of dill (dille)
- 2 cloves of garlic
- 3 tbsp sour cream, e.g. 18% (zure room)

<!-- -->

- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- ½ tsp each of salt and pepper

## Recipe

Prep 20 min · Cook 40 min · Makes 2 kg of soup<br>
Per 100 g: 46 kcal · 6 g carbs (1 g sugars) · 1 g protein · 2 g fat<br>
Gluten-free, low-sugar, vegetarian
{: .recipe-facts }

Pour the vegetable stock into a pot and start heating it. Peel the washed
potatoes, dice them and add them to the stock. Keep it cooking gently over low
heat.

Rinse the chanterelles thoroughly under cold running water and remove any grit.
Halve the larger ones. Pat dry on paper towel. Heat a frying pan, add the butter
and the chanterelles. While they fry, peel the two garlic cloves, slice them and
add them to the mushrooms. After 10 minutes of frying, season with the salt and
pepper.

Tip the entire contents of the pan into the pot with the stock. Peel the carrot,
grate it on the coarse side of a grater and add it to the soup as well. Carry on
cooking until the vegetables are tender. Near the end, stir in the chopped dill.

Turn the heat down to the lowest setting. Put the sour cream in a small bowl, add
a few spoonfuls of the hot soup and stir. Add another half a cup of stock and
stir again. Pour the tempered cream into the soup and stir everything through —
you can also pass it through a sieve as you add it.

## Notes

Fresh chanterelles are seasonal and expensive here; frozen ones from a Polish
shop or a toko work just as well, which the author says herself. Dutch `zure
room` is about 20% against the Polish 18% and is the right thing to buy — crème
fraîche is too rich. Tempering the cream before it goes in is what stops it
splitting, so do not skip it. Check the stock: some Dutch `groentebouillon`
cubes still list animal fat.
