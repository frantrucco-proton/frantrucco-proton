A place to keep things I look up more than once.

**Recipes** come first. Every one of them is vegetarian, lists its ingredients
in both English and Dutch so it can be read in a Dutch supermarket, and links
back to the original it came from. Where a recipe has been changed from that
original — a substitution, an ingredient swapped out — the page says so.

Other sections will follow: Dutch taxes, notes on cities, useful websites for
finding housing.
